SOLMARINA

A SUN-SOAKED OPEN-WORLD CRIME GAME

You are Marco Vela, a courier in Solmarina. Drive, steal, fight and outrun the whole city — three missions, one way out.


WASD move  •  E enter a car  •  F punch  •  Click shoot  •  Esc pause

HOW TO PLAY

On foot: WASD move, Shift sprint, drag the mouse to look around, E enters the nearest car, F punches, click shoots once you have the pistol.

Driving: W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrakes, E gets out. Stealing a car, hitting people and fighting the cops raises your wanted level — lay low and it falls.

Missions: follow the coral beacon and the minimap. Finish all three to beat the game. Your cash and rep save automatically.
